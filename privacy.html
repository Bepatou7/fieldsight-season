FieldSight Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Short version: FieldSight does not collect, store or share any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app shows

FieldSight shows public VEX V5 Robotics Competition information: teams, events, match results, rankings, skills scores and awards. This information comes from the official VEX events database (events.vex.com), where it is already published publicly. FieldSight adds statistics calculated from those results, such as its Power Rating.

Information stored on your device

The teams you follow and your alliance pick lists are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Information sent over the internet

The app downloads public competition files hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open or follow a team, the app downloads that team's public file. These requests do not include your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. We run no server and keep no logs. GitHub, which hosts the files, may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

FieldSight is suitable for students of all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Third parties

FieldSight does not sell or share data, and contains no third-party advertising or analytics software. FieldSight is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by VEX Robotics or the REC Foundation.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
